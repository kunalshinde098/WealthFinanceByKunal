How SIP is calculated

Future value of a monthly SIP

FV = P × [((1 + r)ⁿ − 1) ÷ r]

P is the monthly contribution, r is the effective monthly rate and n is the number of monthly contributions. Contributions are assumed at month-end.

Important: Mutual fund returns are market-linked and not guaranteed. This calculator is educational and does not account for exit loads, expense ratios or taxes.
