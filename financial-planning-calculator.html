How this calculation works

Reverse goal-planning calculation

Existing corpus FV = Current investments × (1 + r)ⁿ; SIP = Gap × r ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

The future value of current investments is deducted from the target. The remaining gap is funded by a month-end SIP. The annual return is converted to an effective monthly rate.

Important: This calculator provides an illustrative estimate, not a guaranteed return or financial advice. Actual returns, tax treatment, product rules and lender terms may differ. Verify current rates and applicable rules before making decisions.
