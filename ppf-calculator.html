How this calculation works

PPF estimate

Each year: Balance = (Previous balance + annual deposit) × (1 + assumed rate)

This simplified model assumes the full annual deposit is made at the start of each year. Actual PPF interest is calculated monthly on eligible balances and rates can change; use the official statement for exact maturity values.
