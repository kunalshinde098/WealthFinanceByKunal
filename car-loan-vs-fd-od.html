Calculation method and assumptions

Net OD cost = OD interest − after-tax FD interest − cashback

The same monthly payment is applied to both financing options. OD interest is serviced first and only the remaining payment reduces OD principal; the simulation continues beyond the original loan term if required. FD interest uses quarterly compounding and a simplified flat tax rate. Cashback is assumed to be received immediately. Actual OD terms, FD lien rules, fees, taxes and reward eligibility can change the result.
