How this calculation works

Retirement corpus calculation

Corpus = Σ [Retirement-month expense × (1 + monthly inflation)ᵐ⁻¹ ÷ (1 + monthly post-retirement return)ᵐ]

The corpus is the value at retirement of monthly expenses that rise with inflation throughout retirement. Current retirement savings are grown to retirement and deducted before calculating the required SIP.

How retirement corpus is calculated

Present value of inflation-adjusted retirement withdrawals

Corpus = Σ [Monthly expense × (1 + monthly inflation)ᵐ⁻¹ ÷ (1 + monthly post-retirement return)ᵐ]

The calculation estimates the corpus needed at retirement to fund monthly withdrawals that increase with inflation. Existing retirement investments are compounded until retirement and offset against the required corpus. The remaining amount is converted into a monthly SIP using the assumed pre-retirement return.
