How this estimate works

  • Regular EMI is calculated from the outstanding balance, interest rate and remaining tenure.
  • Strategy A pays the normal EMI plus the monthly surplus toward the loan principal.
  • Strategy B pays the normal EMI while depositing the surplus into an RD for the selected number of months, then uses the full RD maturity amount — all deposits (principal) plus interest after assumed tax — for a lump-sum principal prepayment.
  • RD deposits are modelled monthly with an approximate monthly rate. Banks may calculate RD interest and maturity differently.
  • The prepayment fee is applied to the amount used to prepay. Check your lender's partial-prepayment rules and charges.
  • This is an estimate, not financial advice or a bank quote. Validate the outputs before making a financial decision.

Calculation method and assumptions

Net cost = Loan interest + prepayment fees − after-tax RD interest

Strategy A pays the monthly surplus directly against loan principal. Strategy B invests the same surplus into an RD and uses its estimated maturity value for a lump-sum prepayment in the selected month. RD is modelled with monthly effective compounding and month-end deposits; actual bank RD compounding, tax and prepayment rules may differ.
