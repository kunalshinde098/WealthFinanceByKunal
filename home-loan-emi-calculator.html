How this calculation works

Loan EMI calculation

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

P is principal, r is monthly interest rate (annual nominal rate ÷ 12), and n is the number of monthly payments. Assumes a fixed rate and regular monthly payments.
